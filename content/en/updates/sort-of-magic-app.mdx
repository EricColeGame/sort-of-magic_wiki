## Troubleshooting and Community Reports

Updates occasionally introduce their own problems. Most are temporary and fixable without reinstalling.

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| App crashes on launch after updating | Corrupted cached data | Force-close, then clear cache in device settings |
| A trick's video will not play | Assets not fully downloaded | Reconnect to Wi-Fi and let downloads finish |
| Gestures feel less responsive | Changed input timing or a low-power mode | Disable battery saver, restart the device |
| Progress or favorites look missing | Sync lag between device and account | Sign out and back in, then wait for sync |
| Update will not install | Insufficient storage | Free space, then retry from the store |

Player experience varies here, so treat these as starting points rather than guarantees. Community reports from long-time users consistently flag two patterns worth remembering: most post-update bugs resolve within a release or two, and the fastest workaround is almost always a clean restart rather than a full reinstall. Reinstalling should be your last resort, since it can wipe locally stored practice data that was never synced.

If a problem persists across multiple devices, report it through the app's feedback option. Specific reports — device model, what you tapped, and what happened next — get fixed far faster than "it's broken."
